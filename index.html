Research Interests

Software Quality, Testing, and Verification

I am interested in how we can systematically find, understand, and prevent bugs in software systems. This includes developing better testing methodologies, improving the usability and effectiveness of static analysis and automated testing tools, and leveraging AI/ML techniques to enhance software quality. I am particularly drawn to how rigorous evaluation methods can verify that software not only works correctly, but is robust, reliable, and resilient to edge cases and unexpected inputs.

Human-Centered Design and User Experience Research

I believe that well-designed software emerges from deep understanding of user needs. I am interested in UI/UX research that involves designing, prototyping, and systematically evaluating user experiences. This includes making developer tools more intuitive and effective, understanding how users interact with data visualizations and interfaces, and applying human-centered interaction modeling to build systems that serve real people better.

Software Engineering

I am interested in how software engineering principles and HCI methods can be applied to solve problems in critical domains like healthcare, education, and social contexts. This includes designing and assessing educational technologies using human-centered methods, engineering software systems that support social good, and understanding how technology impacts human behavior and society particularly in domains like online social networks and data-driven systems.
